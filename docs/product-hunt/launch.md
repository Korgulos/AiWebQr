## First maker comment

Hi Product Hunt, I'm Dejan, the maker of QR Gen Me.

QR Gen Me lets you blend a QR code with a photo or an animated GIF background. This launch focuses on artistic and animated QR codes for designers, event organizers and anyone making a digital invitation or portfolio.

You can create and download a PNG or GIF without an account. An account is needed if you want to save designs to campaigns.

I've included three examples: a moving equalizer for an event screen, a gentle animated invitation, and a geometric portfolio QR. The demo codes open the QR Gen Me homepage; replace that URL with your own when creating a similar design.

A few practical details: backgrounds are cropped to a square, uploads are limited to 5 MB, and the first generation takes longer while the image tools load. Use animated QR codes on screens and static PNGs for print. Always test your downloaded result on real phones.

Which would you use first: an event screen, a digital invitation or a portfolio? I'd love feedback on the workflow and scan reliability.
