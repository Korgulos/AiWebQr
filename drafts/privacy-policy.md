# QR Gen Me privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

## Who operates this service

QR Gen Me at qr-gen-me.com is operated by Dejan Katić. For privacy questions,
account access or deletion requests, contact **korgulos.inc@gmail.com**.

## Account and sign-in data

We store your name, email address, account settings, sign-in timestamps and the
content you save to your account. Passwords created through email registration
are stored as password hashes, not as readable passwords.

If you choose Google sign-in, we request your basic profile and email address.
We store the provider's account identifier, your name and email to create or
recognize your QR Gen Me account. We use this information for sign-in and account
management, not advertising. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive,
contacts or calendars. Google tokens are processed temporarily during sign-in;
they are not saved in our application database.

If Microsoft sign-in is enabled, we similarly use your Microsoft account
identifier, name and available email address for sign-in and account management.
We do not request mailbox, file or calendar access.

## QR codes, campaigns and files

Creating and downloading an unsaved QR code is generally performed in your
browser. Saving a campaign, design or uploaded file sends that content to our
servers. Campaign content may include information you enter about other people;
only submit information you are entitled to use.

Files and saved QR images hosted by the service have public URLs. Anyone with
such a URL can access its content. Campaign tracking links redirect visitors to
the destination configured by the campaign owner.

## Campaign analytics and technical data

When a tracked campaign link opens, we record the time, campaign, browser,
operating system, device category, user-agent and selected request metadata,
including referring domain and language where available. We use a keyed hash
derived from the IP address, user-agent, campaign and day to estimate visits and
detect rapid repeats. The application does not store the raw IP address in new
scan-event records. Hosting providers may separately process IP addresses and
technical logs to deliver and protect the service. Campaign owners can see
aggregated statistics for their campaigns.

## Cookies

The service uses an HttpOnly sign-in cookie lasting up to seven days, temporary
OAuth cookies lasting up to ten minutes, and a time-zone preference cookie lasting
up to one year. These support sign-in, security and displaying local times.

## Service providers and external content

We use Vercel for hosting and file storage and Neon for PostgreSQL storage. These
providers process data to operate the service. Google and Microsoft process
sign-in requests under their own privacy policies. Using optional GIPHY search
or loading external images and other resources sends requests to those providers;
they can receive your IP address and browser information. Opening a destination
encoded in a QR code takes you to that destination's own service and policies.

## Retention and deletion

Account information and saved content are retained while your account remains
active. Campaign scan records do not currently have an automatic expiry and are
removed with the owning campaign or account. You can delete a password-enabled
account in Account settings. If you signed up only through a social provider or
cannot use that flow, contact the address above to request deletion. We may need
to verify that you control the account. Copies in provider-managed backups and
technical logs can remain subject to the providers' retention settings.

You can contact us to request access to or correction of your account information,
or deletion of your account and associated content. Removing Google or Microsoft
access in the provider's settings prevents future use of that authorization but
does not by itself delete your QR Gen Me account.

## Changes

We will update this page when these practices change and show the revision date
above.
